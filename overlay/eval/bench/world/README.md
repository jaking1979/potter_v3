# The Alder household (fictional benchmark world)

Everything here is invented. "Today" in the benchmark is Wednesday, March 10, 2027, 8:00 AM (America/New_York).
- USER.md, MEMORY.md, preferences/, rules/people/: what the assistant knew before the last three weeks.
- data/messages.jsonl: what the owner told the assistant over the last three weeks (two facts are later changed).
- data/inbox.jsonl, calendar.jsonl, tasks.jsonl, house.jsonl: the owner's mail, calendar, tasks and cabin readings.
