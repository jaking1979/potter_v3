# Home

Read live state first and report what matters (heat or freeze risk, doors and locks, leaks, offline devices), with
the time of the reading. Climate plans and device changes the owner asks for are allowed: say what you will
change, do it, then confirm the new state. If Home Assistant is not connected or unreachable, say so and do not
guess values.
